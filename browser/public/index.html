A BETTER FIRST REPAIR

A few simple steps.

Get the most out of your cleanup workspace.

01

Choose your clip

Choose a browser-playable video up to 200 MB, 1080p and 3 minutes for a full export. Processing happens on your device. No upload, processing account or separate server is required.

02

Mark the watermark

Pause at a clear frame and drag a box around the logo or text. For moving or animated marks, choose mask keyframes, scrub to new times, and redraw the box. Add positions at turns and size changes. The mask interpolates between positions; this is manual guidance, not automatic object tracking.

03

Review the result

Use Preview to test three seconds around the playhead before a full export. Detail mode compares repeating patterns, nearby lighting and clean earlier frames. Areas without usable evidence use a smooth estimate. Synthesized areas are estimates. Coverage shows source use, not a guarantee of accurate reconstruction.
